Design Process

01

Research

Studying the fixture, the ongoing narrative, the rivalry, and gathering high-resolution player photography that matches the exact emotional tone.

02

Concept

Building the mood. Structuring the hierarchy of elements to ensure the story hits the viewer immediately before they process the details.

03

Composition

Meticulous player placement, typography integration, and establishing depth through foreground and background environmental elements.

04
3x Upscale for ultra sharp HD.

Final Polish

Lighting is crucial—simulating warm tungsten, a large soft key with gentle falloff, and applying cinematic low-key effects before premium upscale enhancements.
