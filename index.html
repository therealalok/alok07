More About Me

I'm Alok Sharma, a Computer Science student based in Kathmandu, Nepal, currently in my 4th semester of B.Sc.CSIT at Amrit Science Campus (Tribhuvan University). Alongside my studies, I design premium football posters, matchday graphics, and Instagram content built around European football.

My work blends editorial-style compositions, cinematic lighting, and clean typography to turn football moments into shareable visual stories — from Champions League nights to tribute posters for club legends.
